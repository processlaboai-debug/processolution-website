1.1. Our role

We are the controller of your account data (section 2.1), technical logs and your enquiries. When the App is used by an organisation (team), that organisation is the controller of its tasks, contacts, attached files and emails received, and we process this data on its behalf as a processor under Article 28 GDPR and the data processing agreement concluded with the organisation. In that case, please contact your organisation first about your data; we will help it to give effect to your rights.

When you use the App personally rather than on behalf of a company or other organisation (for example, you are the only member of your space and use it for personal purposes), we are the controller of all your data.

In the App every workspace is an organisation, so a single-member organisation is subject to the same rules as a team: the same arrangements apply to the account, tasks, files, export and deletion.

2. What data we collect

2.1. Account data

  • Email address
  • Name (if you provided it or we received it from your Google or Apple account)
  • If you sign in with Apple – the email address provided by Apple, which may be a hidden relay address created by Apple
  • Two-factor authentication data. If a user enables two-factor authentication, the secret key generated for them is stored. One-time codes are verified against it. The dates of activation and of confirmed sign-ins are stored with it. No phone number or other device data is collected for this purpose.
  • Profile photo (only if you sign in with Google)
  • A non-reversible hash of your password (if you registered with email) – we do not store the password itself and cannot recover it
  • Membership of an organisation (team), your role (owner or member) and invitations

2.2. Usage data

  • Tasks you create (text, deadlines, priority, assignees, statuses, comments, re-delegation and status history)
  • Contacts and senders you add in order to assign tasks
  • Voice input. On iOS and Android devices, the App temporarily records audio and sends it to Google Gemini via Lovable for transcription. In supported desktop browsers, the browser's speech recognition service is used. (see section 5). The App stores the transcription, as well as your voice vocabulary, corrections and templates.
  • Data sent to artificial intelligence. To break down a task, Google Gemini (via Lovable) may receive, together with the task text, contact names, your voice vocabulary, correction examples and templates.
  • Notification email subject. When a task has no title, its description text may be sent to Google Gemini via the Lovable AI service to prepare a short summary for the email subject. This processing does not depend on whether the task was entered by text or by voice. If the description contains personal data, that data is also included in the text sent. Refusing or withdrawing permission for voice input does not turn off the preparation of the email subject summary.
  • Records of the organisation owner's acceptance of the terms of use and the data processing agreement: owner and organisation identifiers, document versions, content checksums, document language and the date and time of acceptance.
  • Voice input consent history: date of consent or withdrawal, consent text version and platform (iOS, Android or browser)
  • Files attached to tasks (PDFs, photos, documents). They are kept in private Lovable Cloud storage. When you open a file in the App, a link valid for 10 minutes is created; a link sent in a notification email is valid for up to 30 days. Anyone who has a valid link can open the file without signing in, so do not share links with outsiders. When a task or account is deleted, the attached files are deleted with it.
  • Emails received that are turned into tasks. If your organisation has enabled the "Tasks by email" feature, emails sent to its CloudMailin address (sender address, subject and text; email attachments are not stored) enter the App and become a task or await approval. Pending and rejected emails can only be seen by the organisation owner; a task created from an email can be seen by team members who can see that task. Rejected or unapproved emails are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • SMTP settings you provide for sending emails; team task notifications use the mail provider set by the organisation owner, not each member's own mailbox (email provider, server, port, username and app password, stored encrypted; the encryption key is kept separately from the database)
  • Phone and browser notification (push) settings and the device notification subscription (push token), phone platform (iOS or Android), selected new-task notification and deadline-reminder settings
  • Translation error reports you submit via "Settings → Translation errors"
  • Selected language and settings

2.3. Data of other persons that we do not obtain directly from them

The App also processes data of persons who are not users of the App themselves: contact details of task assignees and requesters (name, email address) entered by users, email addresses of persons invited to an organisation, and data of senders of emails sent to the organisation's "Tasks by email" address. We obtain this data from the user of the App or their organisation. We use it only to assign tasks and to send notifications about tasks. Contact names may be sent to Google Gemini to break down a task (section 2.2). Notification emails contain a link to this privacy policy and an option to unsubscribe from notifications.

2.4. What we do NOT collect

  • Location (GPS)
  • Contacts from your phone
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Payment data

We do not keep audio recordings in the App's database, but when you dictate on iOS and Android devices, audio is temporarily recorded and sent for transcription (section 2.2).

4. How we use data

When you enable task notifications, we send your device a notification about a new task assigned to you and its deadline. You may separately enable one reminder 3, 2 or 1 day before the deadline; reminder timing is checked hourly. These reminders are not sent for completed, accepted, rejected or delegated tasks. A notification may contain the task title, number and deadline; this information may appear on the lock screen depending on your phone settings. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in the App or restricted through phone permissions. Delivery depends on the device, connectivity and provider and is not guaranteed. Data is used to provide the feature you select (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; organisation data is processed under its instructions).

  • Providing the App's features (creating and delegating tasks)
  • Sending email notifications to assignees – via the mail provider set by the organisation owner and, if that fails, via a sending channel managed by Lovable; system reminders and warnings may be sent directly via Lovable (section 5)
  • Speech recognition and breaking down task text (assignee, deadline, priority) using artificial intelligence (Google Gemini via Lovable)
  • Preparing a summary for the notification email subject using artificial intelligence (Google Gemini via Lovable) when a task has no title
  • Security and prevention of unauthorised access
  • Handling requests and questions sent by email to info@processolution.pro (about accounts, sign-in and data)

We NEVER: sell your data, use it for advertising or carry out profiling. We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Article 22 GDPR).

We aim to reply within 7 working days, and in any case no later than within 1 month (Article 12(3) GDPR).

You can also request deletion of your account and data without installing the App: https://task.processolution.pro/delete-account or by email to info@processolution.pro.

7.1. What account deletion means

When you select Settings → Delete account or submit a request, the deletion process starts once the request is received. The following are deleted: your profile, the tasks you created and the files attached to them, contacts, senders, SMTP settings, voice vocabulary, corrections and templates, notification settings and push subscriptions, unaccepted invitations you sent, and your sign-in (auth) account. If part of the data cannot be deleted because of a technical error, the clean-up is repeated. This action is irreversible.

Deleting an account does not delete the whole team. If you are the owner of an organisation and other members remain, the owner's rights pass to another member (the longest-standing one) and the organisation continues to operate. The organisation is deleted only when no other members remain.

For legal or technical reasons, the following may remain:

  • The list of blocked / unsubscribed addresses (legitimate interest)
  • The email sending log – for no longer than 90 days
  • Tasks created by other users in which your email address is listed as an assignee or requester contact – those records belong to the other user's account; to have them removed, contact us or that user
  • Database backups – until the end of the retention period applied by the infrastructure provider

7.2. Leaving a team

When you leave a team (Settings → Team → "Leave team"), the tasks you created and those assigned to you, their history, files and comments remain with the team owner and continue to be visible in their team. You will no longer see them.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for persons aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data of persons under 18. If we learn that we have received such data, we will delete it.

10. Data security

  • Two-factor authentication (6-digit one-time codes from an authenticator app). A user can enable two-factor authentication for their account. The organisation owner, having confirmed sign-in with two-factor authentication, can make it mandatory for the organisation. Until a member enables it, the organisation's tasks and contacts are not shown to them. Members join an organisation only by invitation from the owner
  • TLS encryption of data in transit
  • Stored data is protected by the security measures of the Lovable Cloud infrastructure
  • Account passwords are stored as non-reversible hashes, not as decryptable passwords
  • Access control at database level (Row-Level Security) – each user sees only their own data and the data of their organisation that they are allowed to see. Only the owner sees all of the organisation's tasks; other members see only the tasks they have access to
  • Links to attached files have a limited validity: 10 minutes in the App, up to 30 days in a notification email

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the State Data Protection Inspectorate no later than within 72 hours (Article 33 GDPR), and if the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will also notify you without undue delay (Article 34 GDPR).

  • Pranešimo laiško temos santraukos parengimas dirbtiniu intelektu (Google Gemini per Lovable), kai užduotis neturi pavadinimo
  • Saugumas ir nesankcionuotos prieigos prevencija
  • Prašymų ir klausimų, siunčiamų el. paštu info@processolution.pro, tvarkymas (paskyros, prisijungimo ir duomenų klausimais)

7.1. Ką reiškia paskyros ištrynimas

Pasirinkus Nustatymai → Ištrinti paskyrą arba pateikus prašymą, ištrynimo procesas pradedamas gavus prašymą. Ištrinami: jūsų profilis, jūsų sukurtos užduotys ir prie jų prisegti failai, kontaktai, siuntėjai, SMTP nustatymai, balso žodynas, korekcijos ir šablonai, pranešimų nustatymai bei push prenumeratos, jūsų išsiųsti dar nepriimti kvietimai ir prisijungimo (auth) paskyra. Jei dėl techninės klaidos dalies duomenų ištrinti nepavyksta, valymas kartojamas. Veiksmas negrįžtamas.

Paskyros ištrynimas nėra visos komandos ištrynimas. Jei esate organizacijos savininkas ir organizacijoje lieka kitų narių, savininko teisės perduodamos kitam nariui (seniausiam), o organizacija toliau veikia. Organizacija ištrinama tik tada, kai kitų narių nelieka.

Dėl teisinių ar techninių priežasčių gali likti:

  • Blokuotų / atsisakiusių gauti laiškus adresų sąrašas (teisėtas interesas)
  • El. laiškų siuntimo žurnalas – ne ilgiau kaip 90 dienų
  • Kitų vartotojų sukurtos užduotys, kuriose jūsų el. pašto adresas nurodytas kaip vykdytojo ar užsakovo kontaktas – tie įrašai priklauso kito vartotojo paskyrai; norėdami juos pašalinti, kreipkitės į mus arba į tą vartotoją
  • Duomenų bazės atsarginės kopijos – iki infrastruktūros tiekėjo taikomo saugojimo laikotarpio pabaigos

7.2. Išėjimas iš komandos

Išėjus iš komandos (Nustatymai → Komanda → „Palikti komandą“), jūsų sukurtos ir jums priskirtos užduotys, jų istorija, failai ir komentarai lieka komandos savininkui ir toliau matomi jo komandoje. Jūs jų nebematysite.

Programėlė naudoja tik būtinus slapukus ir įrenginio vietinę saugyklą: prisijungimo sesijos tokeną, kalbos pasirinkimą, UI nustatymus. Rinkodaros ar analitinių slapukų bei sekimo technologijų NENAUDOJAME.

9. Vaikų privatumas

Programėlė skirta 18 metų ir vyresniems asmenims. Sąmoningai nerenkame jaunesnių nei 18 metų asmenų duomenų. Jei sužinosime, kad tokių duomenų gavome, juos ištrinsime.

10. Duomenų saugumas

  • Dviguba autentifikacija (6 skaitmenų vienkartiniai kodai iš autentifikatoriaus programėlės). Naudotojas gali įjungti dvigubą autentifikaciją savo paskyrai. Organizacijos savininkas, patvirtinęs prisijungimą dviguba autentifikacija, gali nustatyti ją kaip privalomą organizacijai. Kol narys jos neįsijungia, jam nerodomos organizacijos užduotys ir kontaktai. Nariai į organizaciją patenka tik pagal savininko kvietimą
  • TLS šifravimas duomenims perduodant
  • Saugomiems duomenims taikomos Lovable Cloud infrastruktūros saugumo priemonės
  • Paskyrų slaptažodžiai saugomi kaip neatkuriamos maišos (hash), o ne iššifruojami slaptažodžiai
  • Prieigos kontrolė duomenų bazės lygiu (Row-Level Security) – kiekvienas vartotojas mato tik savo ir savo organizacijos duomenis, kuriuos jam leidžiama matyti. Visas organizacijos užduotis mato tik jos savininkas; kiti nariai mato tik tas užduotis, prie kurių turi prieigą
  • Prisegtų failų nuorodos riboto galiojimo: programėlėje – 10 minučių, pranešimo laiške – iki 30 dienų

Įvykus asmens duomenų saugumo pažeidimui, apie jį pranešime Valstybinei duomenų apsaugos inspekcijai ne vėliau kaip per 72 valandas (BDAR 33 str.), o jei pažeidimas gali kelti didelį pavojų jūsų teisėms ir laisvėms – nedelsdami pranešime ir jums (BDAR 34 str.).
